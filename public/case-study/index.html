Project Overview

About the Project

Lasertronics E-Shop is a high-performance e-commerce platform for Lasertronics (Pvt) Ltd., serving electronics engineers, researchers and technicians across Sri Lanka.

Industry Electronics
Project Type E-Commerce Platform
Outcome Faster, simpler & more reliable online purchasing

The Challenge

  • Manual counter recordkeeping for 1,200+ SKUs
  • Order handling and stock updates were time-consuming
  • Pricing updates were often delayed

Our Solution

ValGrow Labs developed an integrated e-commerce platform with real-time InTouch ERP sync, instant search and WhatsApp checkout, giving Lasertronics a faster, more accurate and customer-friendly digital store.
